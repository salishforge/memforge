# MemForge — Official LongMemEval Results

Paper-comparable end-to-end QA accuracy. **Hand-maintained** — unlike
`RESULTS.md`, which is regenerated by `report.ts` on every benchmark run and
will silently discard anything written into it.

## Protocol

- Dataset: LongMemEval-S, all 500 questions, no sampling.
- Judge and reader: `gpt-4o-2024-08-06` (LongMemEval's protocol judge).
- Configuration: **defaults** — hybrid retrieval, `limit=10`, whole memories,
  no snippet budget, no reranking. A tuned configuration is not reported as the
  headline; see "Configuration sensitivity" below for why that matters.
- Corpus: one session per warm row (`CONSOLIDATION_INNER_BATCH_SIZE=1`), so
  rows and sessions are 1:1 and recall over rows equals recall over sessions.
- Run completes only at 500/500 with zero errors; partial runs are discarded.

## Results

| Run | Date | QA accuracy | Avg score | Context p50 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| v4 baseline | 2026-07-29 | **67.6%** | 70.6% | 32,396 tok |
| (superseded) | 2026-07-28 | 58.4% | 61.4% | 32,466 tok |

The 2026-07-28 figure was measured through two harness defects — keyword-only
retrieval instead of hybrid, and no session dates supplied to the reader. It is
retained only to show their size and **should not be cited**.

### Per category

| category | n | 07-28 | 07-29 (current) | delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| single-session-assistant | 56 | 94.6% | 96.4% | +1.8pp |
| knowledge-update | 78 | 70.5% | 76.9% | +6.4pp |
| single-session-user | 70 | 78.6% | 75.7% | −2.9pp |
| multi-session | 133 | 51.9% | 59.4% | +7.5pp |
| single-session-preference | 30 | 56.7% | 56.7% | 0.0pp |
| temporal-reasoning | 133 | 32.3% | 56.4% | +24.1pp |

`temporal-reasoning` accounts for most of the gain and is a harness correction
rather than a retrieval improvement: those questions ask elapsed time between
events, and the reader was previously given no dates at all, so they were
unanswerable by construction.

`single-session-user` lost ground, consistent with the known residual where
equal-weight RRF fusion still occasionally buries a session the lexical arm
ranked first.

## Supporting retrieval metrics

Same corpus, hybrid mode, all 500 questions:

| metric | value |
|---|---:|
| Recall@1 | 83.8% |
| Recall@5 | 95.4% |
| Recall@10 | 97.6% |
| **Complete-evidence recall** | **91.8%** |
| Query latency p50 | 46 ms |

Complete-evidence recall — every gold session present, not merely one — is the
honest ceiling for what a reader can answer. The gap between it (91.8%) and QA
(67.6%) is reading and synthesis, not retrieval.

## Configuration sensitivity

Identical retrieval, three reader families, 500 questions each. The optimal
context width differs *in direction* between models, which is why defaults are
reported rather than a tuned configuration:

| configuration | ctx p50 | gpt-oss:120b | deepseek-v4-flash |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| whole memories, k=10 (default) | 32,466 | 58.4% | **69.8%** |
| whole memories, k=5 | 16,711 | 62.8% | 67.6% |
| passages, 1500 tok/memory | 14,083 | **67.2%** | 67.4% |
| passages, 3000 tok/memory | 25,889 | 61.8% | 69.4% |
| passages, 200 tok/memory | 4,081 | 51.8% | 49.2% |

Weaker readers peak on roughly 14k tokens of extracted passages; the strongest
reader peaks on 32k of whole memories and loses ground when narrowed. Llama-3.3-70B
tracked the weaker pattern (56.2% at k=10, 62.6% at k=5).

Reranking (retrieve 10, rerank, show 5) reached 64.6% for gpt-oss at 16,496
tokens, but adds ~11.5s per query. Reranking combined with passage extraction
scored 56.4% — worse than either alone, as two lossy stages compound.

## Reproducing

```bash
BENCHMARK_LIMIT=500 npx tsx benchmarks/longmemeval/ingest.ts
BENCHMARK_LIMIT=500 BENCHMARK_MODES=hybrid npx tsx benchmarks/longmemeval/evaluate.ts
QA_API_BASE=https://api.openai.com/v1 QA_API_KEY=... \
  QA_JUDGE_MODEL=gpt-4o-2024-08-06 QA_READER_MODEL=gpt-4o-2024-08-06 \
  npx tsx benchmarks/longmemeval-qa/evaluate.ts
```

A full official run costs roughly $35–40 in API usage and about 15 minutes,
after a ~45 minute corpus ingest.
